Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

H2N to Sheets is a personal desktop utility used to synchronize selected poker statistics and metadata from Hand2Note with Google Sheets.

Google account data

H2N to Sheets uses Google OAuth authorization to access Google Sheets explicitly authorized by the user.

The application may receive OAuth access and refresh tokens required to perform authorized Google Sheets operations. These credentials are stored locally on the user's computer and are not sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.

Data use

Google user data is used only to provide the spreadsheet synchronization functionality requested by the user.

H2N to Sheets does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, marketing, or resale.

Data storage

H2N to Sheets is a local desktop application. Application credentials and runtime data are stored locally on the user's computer except for data intentionally written to Google Sheets through the Google Sheets API.

Data sharing

H2N to Sheets does not sell or share Google user data with third parties.

Revoking access

Users can revoke H2N to Sheets access to their Google account at any time through their Google Account security settings.
